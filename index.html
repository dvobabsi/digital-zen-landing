Block sites automatically, on your schedule

Create focus periods for any time range and any days of the week. Morning routine, work hours, study time — Digital Zen enforces the schedule so you don't have to.

Block, warn, or whitelist — you choose

Full block closes the tab instantly. Warning mode shows a reminder you can dismiss. Whitelist mode lets only your approved sites through — everything else is blocked.
